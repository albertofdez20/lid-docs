# Create a product

A product on Lid is anything digital you can deliver with a file or a link: an ebook, a template, a guide, access to a private folder. Courses have their own flow: see [Courses](courses.md).

![The first step of New product on Lid: "What do buyers get?", with a choice between files and a link, and a preview of how buyers will see the product.](../.gitbook/assets/create-product-step-1.png)

## Steps

1. Open **My store** in your [dashboard](https://lid.pro/dashboard) and click **New product**.
2. Choose what buyers get. Upload files (PDF, ZIP, video) or paste a link, like a Notion page or a shared folder. Only buyers see it, and Lid delivers it the moment they pay.
3. Add a title. Concrete beats clever.
4. Write the description. Say what the buyer gets and who it is for. Lida can draft it for you with **Draft it**.
5. Set the price in US dollars, or choose **Free**.
6. Set the commission you share with people who bring you sales. Anywhere from 1% to 50%; it starts at 10%. A common setup: you keep 87%, the sharer gets 10%, Lid gets 3%.
7. Choose a refund window. With none, you are paid at once. See [Refunds and protection](../sell-on-lid/refunds-and-protection.md).
8. Click **Publish**. Your page is live.

## Optional extras

* **Free previews.** Add files anyone can open before buying, next to the private files only buyers unlock.
* **Co-creators.** Split your share of every sale with other creators, in the same transaction.
* **Visibility.** List the product in Explore, or keep it unlisted so only people with the link find it.
* **A second language.** Add an English or Spanish version of the title and description.

## FAQ

<details>

<summary>Can I edit after publishing?</summary>

Yes. Title, description, price, images, delivery files and commission stay editable from **My store**, and buyers who already paid keep their access. A new price applies to the next sales; past purchases stay as they were.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does Lid charge?</summary>

A flat 3% on every sale, shown up front, and $0 a month to sell.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I get paid?</summary>

In seconds, automatically, in digital dollars. See [Getting paid](payouts-and-usdc.md).

</details>
