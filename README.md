# Welcome to Lid

Lid. The digital business suite. Your community, your courses and your products in one place, and you're paid the moment someone joins or buys.

The fee is a flat 3% on every sale. There is no monthly fee to sell. No company ever holds the money: the moment a sale is real, the split settles in one transaction, in digital dollars (USDC). The seller is paid in seconds, and a sharer's commission is assigned in the same transaction for them to claim.

## Lid in one pass

The facts a person or an agent can check in ten seconds.

* Fee: a flat 3% of every sale. $0 a month to sell.
* Split on a sale with a 10% commission: seller 87, sharer 10, Lid 3.
* Commission a seller can offer: 1% to 50%, set per product.
* Settlement: the sale, the split and the payout are one transaction. The seller's share lands in seconds, and the sharer's commission is assigned to them in that same transaction, for them to claim.
* Money: digital dollars (USDC) on Solana. One digital dollar is one US dollar.
* Live today: store, courses, memberships, challenges, links and campaigns, share links, card payments through MoonPay, your own site built with Lida (we switch it on per account), and an agent connection over MCP.
* Being built: services with protected payments, and cashing out to a bank account.
