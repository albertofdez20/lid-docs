# Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for the issues people hit most. If yours is missing, email [alberto@lid.pro](mailto:alberto@lid.pro).

## I shared my link and the sale didn't count

The buyer has to open your link before they buy. If they reached the product some other way, it counts as a direct sale and there is no commission to pay you.

One common cause: some apps open links in their own built-in browser, and that can break the connection between your link and the sale. Ask your buyer to open the link in a normal browser like Safari or Chrome, then buy.

Commissions cannot be added to a sale after it happens, so share the link itself, everywhere.

## My money isn't showing

Check your balance first. A seller's payout lands within seconds of a sale. If you shared the link, your commission waits in **Earnings & analytics → Affiliates** until you withdraw it.

If the product has a refund window, the money is held until the window closes or the buyer releases it. That is normal, and it releases automatically.

If it has been longer than a few minutes and nothing shows, contact us and we will look right away.

## I can't log in

Lid signs you in with your email. Request a login code, then check your inbox and your spam folder. Make sure you are using the same email you signed up with. Still stuck? Email us from that address.

## My buyer wants a refund

If you set a refund window on the product, the buyer can open a dispute inside that window. A person on our team looks at it and either releases the money to you or returns the full amount to the buyer, including any commission from the sale. After the window closes, the sale is final. If you set no window, sales are final right away.

If something needs a human decision, email us and we will help.

## My card payment didn't go through

Card payments run through MoonPay, which checks your identity before your first card payment and only works where it operates. If it does not work for you, add USDC on Solana from any exchange or wallet app instead, or email us.

## I sent the wrong coin

Your Lid balance holds USDC on Solana only. If you sent another coin, or used another network, email us with the details before you send anything else.
