# Vision

**Every digital business runs on Lid.**

Lid is the creator suite: store, challenges, campaigns and memberships in one place, and the money settles the second a result is real. Live on Solana.

## The problem: the bleed and the wait

Five tools, two months, twenty percent gone.

Sell $100K a year through today's stack: $15,000 to $20,000 gone in fees. Then sixty days for the rest. Gumroad takes 10. Patreon takes 10. OnlyFans takes 20. The software underneath charges $1,900 to $7,600 a year on top. On the funding side, performance networks take 5 to 30% of spend and hold it 30 to 60 days.

## The solution

The whole stack, one place, flat 3%. Store, courses, services, memberships, challenges, campaigns, share links, and a connection for agents. One transaction underneath: the sale, the split and the payout. No company ever holds the money.

Every buyer can share, every sharer earns, every earner can open a store. Each sale recruits the next seller.

## What only Lid does

The only suite where the sale, the split and the payout are one transaction. Budgets sit in a pool the funder controls until the result verifies; the moment it does, the split settles in the same transaction. Not holding the money is the consequence.

Anyone can cut a fee. Whop's processing-only price already matches ours: 2.7% plus $0.30 is 3.0% on a $100 sale. What nobody can cut is their own income statement, and 3% here buys the whole suite. Elsewhere it buys a checkout.

## Why now

Digital dollars went mainstream; commerce is catching up. Three facts:

1. **\~$310B in stablecoins** in circulation (June 2026), headed toward \~$420B by year-end.
2. **The GENIUS Act** (signed July 2025) gave stablecoins their first US federal framework.
3. **AI agents moved \~$73M across \~176M transactions** in the last year. A spender that did not exist two years ago.

## The model, in three sentences

For the individual creator, 3% of every sale and $0 a month, always. For whoever pays for results, a fee on the budget. For the business with a team, a plan a month for the team tools. The three never merge, and the individual creator never pays the other two.

## The principle

Everything at Lid flows from one principle. If a decision doesn't help people earn fairly and grow over time, we don't make it.

## The ladder

Businesses with an audience and the creators around them. Then every business that pays for results. Then every digital business, human or agent.

Businesses with an audience first, and the creators around them: hardest to serve, fastest to move. Agents already transact on the rail.

## What we will not do

* We will not become a 10%+ tollbooth that punishes top earners.
* We will not chase hype or inflate the story beyond what proof supports.
* We will not expose Web3 complexity to end users.
* We will not hold user money. Ever.
* We will not charge the individual creator a monthly fee.

## In one sentence

Lid is the creator suite: run, monetize, and distribute your digital business in one place, and everyone is paid the second a result is real.
