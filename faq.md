# FAQ

The questions people ask most, in one place.

## Your site

<details>

<summary>Can Lid build my website?</summary>

Yes. Tell Lida, Lid's AI, what you do and she writes and designs your site. You see it before you pay, and you publish it on a Lid address or on your own domain. The steps are on [Build your site with Lida](start-here/build-your-site.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is Lid for doctors, dentists and other professionals?</summary>

Yes. Lid gives a professional a website under their own name. Tell Lida what you do and she writes and designs it, and it goes live on your own domain.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Who can build a site?</summary>

Anyone who signs up, with Lida on lid.pro or from an AI assistant over MCP.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Who owns the site?</summary>

You do. It lives in your account, on your domain.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does a site cost?</summary>

You see your site before you pay. Putting a site live is paid, per site, and Lid shows you the price before you pay.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change my site later?</summary>

Yes, any time. Open **Sites**, press **Open in the Builder**, tell Lida what to change, and publish when you like the preview.

</details>

## Selling

<details>

<summary>What can I sell on Lid?</summary>

Digital products, courses and memberships. You can also run challenges with prizes, and put it all on [your own site](sell-on-lid/your-own-site.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What does selling on Lid cost?</summary>

3% of each sale. There is no setup cost.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How fast do I get paid?</summary>

Within seconds of the sale. The payment splits automatically the moment it happens, and your share lands in your balance. If you set a refund window, it waits until the window ends.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need to know anything technical?</summary>

No. You sign up with your email, set your price in dollars, and share your link. Everything else happens automatically.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can other people help me sell?</summary>

Yes. Turn on commission for your product and anyone can grab a share link. You choose the commission, from 1% to 50% of each sale.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change the commission later?</summary>

Yes, going forward. Sales that already happened keep the split they were made under.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can buyers get refunds?</summary>

You choose a refund window when you create a product. Inside that window a buyer can open a dispute, and a person on our team decides whether the money goes to you or back to them. If you set no window, you are paid instantly and sales are final.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I sell memberships or subscriptions?</summary>

Yes. A membership gives your members posts only they can see, messages with you and your own page, on a monthly or yearly plan billed as a recurring subscription. See [Memberships](sell-on-lid/memberships.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I own my customer relationships?</summary>

Yes. Your buyers, your content, and your pricing are yours. Lid is the tool underneath.

</details>

## Sharing and earning

<details>

<summary>How do I earn by sharing?</summary>

Find a product with commission turned on, grab your share link, and post it anywhere. When someone buys through your link, your commission is assigned to you in the same transaction, and only you can claim it.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How much can I earn per sale?</summary>

The seller sets the commission, anywhere from 1% to 50% of the price. You can see it before you share.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need approval to share a product?</summary>

No. If commission is on, anyone can share and earn. There is no application and no waiting.

</details>

<details>

<summary>When do I get my commission?</summary>

It is assigned to you the moment the sale happens. Withdraw it from the **Affiliates** tab under **Earnings** when you want. There is no payout day and no minimum balance.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I earn for inviting people to Lid?</summary>

Yes. Open **My referrals** from the menu under your balance, at the top right, and share your invite link. Everyone who signs up with it is tied to you, and their activity earns you 0.50%. Our team pays referral earnings to you in digital dollars (USDC).

</details>

<details>

<summary>What happens if the buyer gets a refund?</summary>

The whole payment returns to the buyer, including your commission. Refunds undo the full sale for everyone.

</details>

## Buying

<details>

<summary>What do I need to buy something?</summary>

An email address and digital dollars (USDC) to pay with. Your purchases stay in your account.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I pay?</summary>

With digital dollars (USDC). If you don't have any, add them with a card from **Settings → Wallet → Add funds**. The card provider may ask you to verify your identity the first time.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I get a refund?</summary>

If the seller set a refund window, you can open a dispute inside it. If our team decides in your favor, the full amount returns to you.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is my payment protected?</summary>

If the product has a refund window, your payment waits in a hold until the window closes, and you can dispute it in that time. Without a window, your payment goes straight to the right people the second it happens, and nobody can change that afterward.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Where do I find what I bought?</summary>

Log in at [lid.pro](https://lid.pro) with the email you used at checkout. Your purchases are in your account, at [My purchases](https://lid.pro/dashboard/purchases).

</details>

## Lid and AI

<details>

<summary>What is Lida?</summary>

Lida is Lid's AI, and she opens as your home when you sign in. You focus on your business. Lida helps with the rest: she answers your questions, builds your site and changes it when you ask, drafts your products and reads your sales.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can AI agents use Lid?</summary>

Yes. If you run your own AI agent, it can build a site, create products, run challenges, share other people's products, and read its sales through a connection Lid provides. If that means nothing to you, you can ignore it. Details are in [Agents on Lid](agents/).

</details>
