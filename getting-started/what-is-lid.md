# What is Lid

Lid. The business suite. Lid (lid.pro) is the suite for businesses: run, monetize, and distribute your business in one place. Your community, your courses and your products sit in one place, and you're paid the moment someone joins or buys, with a flat 3% per sale and $0 a month to sell. Lid is made by Lid Labs Technologies, in Dubai.

Anyone else can share your link too. When their share leads to a sale, they earn a commission you set, from 1% to 50%.

## Who it's for

Anyone who sells digital things to an audience: a community, an agency, a school, a creator with a team, or one creator on their own. Also anyone with an audience who wants to earn by sharing what others make.

## The four steps

| Step         | What happens                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Create**   | Set up your product in minutes.                                                                                                   |
| **Share**    | Post your link. Anyone can share it too.                                                                                          |
| **Sell**     | Someone buys through any of those links.                                                                                          |
| **Get paid** | You are paid in seconds, in digital dollars. Whoever shared has their commission assigned in the same transaction, and claims it. |

## What makes Lid different

* Everything in one place: store, courses, memberships, challenges, campaigns, share links and your own site (we switch it on per account). The whole stack, flat 3%.
* Anyone can share your product and earn. No applications, no approvals.
* The sale, the split and the payout are one transaction. You are paid in seconds, a sharer's commission is assigned to them in that same transaction, and Lid never holds your money.
* The fee is a flat 3% per sale. $0 a month to sell.
* You never handle anything technical. An email is enough.
* You focus on your business. Lida helps with the rest: she answers your questions, drafts your products and reads your sales; where the Builder is switched on, she builds your site.

AI agents can create and read on Lid too. If that means nothing to you, you can ignore it. If it does, start at [Agents on Lid](../agents/).

## What you can sell today

Digital products, courses and memberships. You can also run challenges with prizes, reward campaigns, and share links that pay commission, and put it all on [your own site](../sell-on-lid/your-own-site.md), where we switch it on for your account. Services with protected payments are being built.
