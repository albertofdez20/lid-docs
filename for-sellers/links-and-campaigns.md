# Reward campaigns

Lid no longer offers reward campaigns. Two pieces do that job today.

* **To pay people for the sales they bring**, give your product a commission. Anyone can share it, and their commission is assigned to them in the same transaction as the sale. See [How sharing and earning works](../for-affiliates/how-affiliate-works.md).
* **To reward your community for a task**, run a challenge with a prize. See [Challenges](bounties.md).
