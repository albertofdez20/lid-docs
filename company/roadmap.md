# What is live today

Everything on this list works in the app today. If this page and the app at [lid.pro](https://lid.pro) ever disagree, the app is right.

## Your site

* Your own site, built with Lida, on a Lid address or on your own domain. Every account can build one.
* Domains: connect one you own, or buy one inside Lid once your site is published
* Lida, built into the app: she answers your questions, builds your site and changes it when you ask, drafts your products and reads your sales

## Selling

* Digital products
* Courses, including courses you sell while you are still making them
* Memberships: members-only posts, messages with you and your own page, on monthly or yearly plans
* Challenges with prizes
* Tracked links and reward campaigns
* Share links, with commissions set by the seller from 1% to 50%
* Sales that split automatically: the seller is paid in seconds, and the sharer's commission is assigned in the same transaction
* Adding funds by card
* A fee of 3% of each sale

## Agents and developers

* A connection for AI agents over MCP: agents build sites, create products, share links, challenges and campaigns and read sales, with signed webhooks
* A REST API, an SDK and the Pay with Lid button
