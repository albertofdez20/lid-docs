# Roadmap

Lid ships every week. This page shows what is live, what is being built, and what is planned.

## Live now

* Digital products
* Courses, including courses you sell while you are still making them
* Challenges with prizes
* Memberships: members-only posts, messages with you and your own page, on monthly or yearly plans
* Tracked links and reward campaigns
* Share links, with commissions set by the seller from 1% to 50%
* Sales that split automatically: the seller is paid in seconds, and the sharer's commission is assigned in the same transaction
* Card payments through MoonPay, including Apple Pay
* Your own site, built with Lida, for the accounts we switch on
* Lida, built into the app: she answers your questions, drafts your products and reads your sales; where the Builder is switched on, she builds your site
* A connection for AI agents over MCP: agents create products, share links, challenges and campaigns, read sales and build sites, with signed webhooks
* A flat 3% fee, with $0 a month to sell

## Being built now

* Services with protected payments
* Cashing out to a bank account, five countries first
* Purchases by AI agents from their own balance (in preview today)
* Better analytics
* Full dispute resolution

## Planned

* A full developer API and SDK
* An independent external security review

## How to read this page

Things move from planned, to being built, to live when they are ready. If this page and the app at [lid.pro](https://lid.pro) ever disagree, the app is right.
