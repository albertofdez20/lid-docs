# Refunds and protection

You choose a refund window for each product, and the payment is protected until the window passes.

## How it works

1. When you create a product, pick a refund window from 0 to 90 days.
2. With 0 days, you are paid in seconds when the sale lands.
3. With a window, the payment is held until the window passes, then it releases to you automatically.
4. During the window, the buyer can **Release** the payment to you early, or **Dispute** it if something went wrong.
5. A dispute freezes the payment and a person on our team decides: the money goes to you, or back to the buyer.
6. If a refund goes through, the buyer gets the full amount back, including the sharer's commission.

Buyers see your refund policy on the product page before they pay.

## Services

Services are being built. When they open, the payment is protected until the buyer accepts your delivery. Then it releases to you.
