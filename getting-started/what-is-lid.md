# What is Lid

Lid. The business suite. Lid (lid.pro) is where a business gets its website and gets paid. Tell Lida, Lid's AI, what you do and she writes and designs your site, on your own domain. Anyone who signs up can build, on lid.pro or from an AI assistant over MCP. The same account sells memberships, courses and digital products, and Lid keeps 3% of each sale. Lid is made by Lid Labs Technologies, in Dubai.

## Who it's for

Professionals of every kind and small business owners who want a website: a dentist, a lawyer, a studio, a shop. Also anyone who sells digital things to an audience: a community, an agency, a school, a creator. And anyone with an audience who wants to earn by sharing the digital products others make.

## How it starts: your site

| Step                      | What happens                                                          |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tell Lida what you do** | One sentence about your business is enough.                           |
| **See your site**         | Lida puts it together. You ask for changes in your own words.         |
| **Put it live**           | You publish when you like it, on a Lid address or on your own domain. |

The step by step is on [Build your site with Lida](../start-here/build-your-site.md).

## When you want to sell online

It is all in the same account. A sale on Lid has four steps:

| Step         | What happens                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Create**   | Set up your product in minutes.                                                                                                   |
| **Share**    | Post your link. Anyone can share it too.                                                                                          |
| **Sell**     | Someone buys through any of those links.                                                                                          |
| **Get paid** | You are paid in seconds, in digital dollars. Whoever shared has their commission assigned in the same transaction, and claims it. |

Anyone else can share your product's link. When their share leads to a sale, they earn a commission you set, from 1% to 50%.

## What makes Lid different

* Your site and your sales in one account: a site built by Lida, a store, courses, memberships, challenges, campaigns and share links.
* Anyone can share your digital product and earn. No applications, no approvals.
* The sale, the split and the payout are one transaction. You are paid in seconds, a sharer's commission is assigned to them in that same transaction, and Lid never holds your money.
* The fee on a sale is 3%.
* You never handle anything technical. An email is enough.
* You focus on your business. Lida helps with the rest: she answers your questions, builds your site and changes it when you ask, drafts your products and reads your sales.

AI agents can create and read on Lid too. If that means nothing to you, you can ignore it. If it does, start at [Agents on Lid](../agents/).
