# Your own site

Your site with your brand, hosted by Lid. Your products are paid through the Lid checkout, the same 3% on every sale.

**How it works.** Open **Builder** in your dashboard and describe the site you want to Lida. She builds it; you change what you like; you publish it on a Lid address or on your own domain.

**What runs on it.** Your store, your courses, your memberships, your challenges and your share links, with the same checkout as your Lid page. You choose which pieces the site shows, and it can also carry a contact form or a waitlist.

**Your own domain.** Connect a domain you own by adding the DNS records you are given: a CNAME record and a TXT record named `_lid-verify`, plus an A record if it is a root domain. The domain goes live once those records check out.

**Your admin.** Every site comes with its own owner admin at `/admin`.

**Who can use it today.** We switch it on per account. If you do not see Builder in your dashboard, write to hello@lid.pro.
