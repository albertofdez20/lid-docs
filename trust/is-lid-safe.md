# Is Lid safe?

Short version: nobody at Lid can touch your money, because Lid never holds it. Here is how that works.

## Who holds the money

Nobody. When a buyer pays, the payment splits the second it happens. The seller gets their share. The person who shared the link has their commission assigned to them, and only they can claim it. Lid gets a flat 3%. The sale, the split and the payout are one transaction. Unless the seller set a refund window, there is no waiting period and no payout day. Your balance is yours the moment you earn it.

## Why the split can't be changed later

The split rules are set on each product, and everyone can see them before they share or buy. Once a sale happens, the record of that sale is permanent and public. Nobody can go back and change who got paid, including Lid.

## What protects buyers

Sellers can set a refund window on their products. During that window, the money is held to one side. The buyer can release it to the seller early, or open a dispute; a person on our team then decides whether it goes to the seller or back to the buyer, and it can go nowhere else. Services, which are being built, will release only when the buyer confirms the work was delivered. Payments become final when both sides are done.

## What protects funders

When you fund a challenge or a campaign, the budget sits in a pool you control until the result verifies. The moment it does, the split settles in the same transaction. Until then, nobody else can move it.

## Is this crypto?

Lid runs on Solana, which is what lets payments split and settle in seconds. You never have to touch, understand, or own any of it. You sign up with your email, you see prices in dollars, and you get paid in digital dollars (USDC). One USDC is worth one US dollar.

## What is still being built

Cashing out to a bank account and services are being built. Card payments are already live through MoonPay. The technology behind Lid is reviewed continuously, and an independent external security review is planned.
