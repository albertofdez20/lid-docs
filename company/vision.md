# Vision

**Every digital business runs on Lid.**

Lid is the creator suite: store, challenges, campaigns, and share links in one place, and the money settles the second a result is real. Live on Solana.

## The problem: the bleed and the wait

Five tools, two months, twenty percent gone.

Sell $100K a year through today's stack: $15,000 to $20,000 gone in fees. Then sixty days for the rest. Gumroad takes 10. Patreon takes 10. OnlyFans takes 20. The software underneath charges $1,900 to $7,600 a year on top. On the funding side, performance networks take 5 to 30% of spend and hold it 30 to 60 days.

## The principle

Everything at Lid flows from one principle. If a decision doesn't help people earn fairly and grow over time, we don't make it.

## Why now

Digital dollars went mainstream; commerce is catching up. Three facts:

1. **\~$310B in stablecoins** in circulation (June 2026), headed toward \~$420B by year-end.
2. **The GENIUS Act** (signed July 2025) gave stablecoins their first US federal framework.
3. **AI agents moved \~$73M across \~176M transactions** in the last year. A spender that did not exist two years ago.

## How Lid wins

**Everything in one place.** Store, challenges, campaigns, share links, memberships. The whole stack, one place, flat 3%.

**Anyone can share and earn.** No approval queue. Every buyer is one share link away from being a sharer, and every sharer is one product away from opening a store.

**Nobody holds the money.** No company ever holds the money. A budget sits set aside, in the funder's control, where everyone can see it, until the result is verified; the moment it is, everyone is paid in the same instant.

**Flat 3%.** 3% of every dollar that moves. $0 of every month. Incumbents can cut a fee. They cannot cut their own income statement.

**Software feel.** Sign up with an email, see prices in dollars, get paid in digital dollars. The blockchain stays invisible.

## The ladder

Creators, then creator businesses, then every digital business.

Creators first. Hardest to serve, fastest to move. Agents already transact on the rail.

## What we will not do

* We will not become a 10%+ tollbooth that punishes top earners.
* We will not chase hype or inflate narrative beyond what proof supports.
* We will not expose Web3 complexity to end users.
* We will not hold user money. Ever.
* We will not introduce subscription tiers until features justify them.

## Business model

A flat 3% fee on every transaction. No subscriptions today. A Pro tier is planned when advanced features justify it.

## In one sentence

Lid is the creator suite: run, monetize, and distribute your digital business in one place, and everyone is paid the second a result is real.
