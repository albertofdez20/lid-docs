# Your own site

Your site with your brand, built with Lida and hosted by Lid. Anyone who signs up can build one. If you sell on it, your products are paid through the Lid checkout, the same 3% on every sale.

New here? [Build your site with Lida](../start-here/build-your-site.md) takes you through it step by step.

**How it works.** Open **Sites** in your dashboard menu, press **Create site** and tell Lida what your site is about. She writes and designs it. You ask for changes in your own words and see them in a preview that only you can see. You press **Publish** when you like it. Lida never publishes for you. To change it later, open **Sites** and press **Open in the Builder** on your site.

**What runs on it.** Your pages, and the pieces you turn on: products, courses, challenges, memberships, a contact form or a waitlist. The pieces that sell use the same checkout as your Lid page. Turning a piece on or off reaches your site in under a minute, without publishing again.

**Your own domain.** Your site starts on a Lid address. In the site's **Domain** tab you can connect a domain you own by adding the DNS records you are given: a CNAME record and a TXT record named `_lid-verify`, plus an A record if it is a root domain. The domain goes live once those records check out, certificate included. Once the site is published you can also buy a domain in that same tab, and Lid connects it for you.

**Your admin.** Every site comes with its own owner admin at `/admin`: your sales, your contacts, your waitlist and your members.

**People.** In the **People** tab you can invite up to 5 editors by email. Editors can change the site and publish it. The money, the domain and the site's admin stay yours. An invitation expires after 14 days if nobody accepts it. From that tab you can also transfer the site to another owner, or ask the Lid team for help.

**The price.** Putting a site live is paid, per site. You see the price in the Builder before you pay, and on [lid.pro](https://lid.pro).

**From an AI assistant.** An AI assistant with a key from your account can build and publish your site over MCP. See [Connect an agent](../agents/connect.md).
