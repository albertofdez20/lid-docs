# Buying on Lid

Someone shared a link with you, or you found something you want. This page walks you through what happens when you buy.

## What happens when you buy

1. Open the product page and select **Buy now**. Before you sign in, the button reads **Sign in to buy**.
2. Sign in with your email. Lid creates an account for you on the spot.
3. Pay with digital dollars (USDC). The price on the page is the amount you pay.
4. You get access immediately. Files are ready to download, and courses open right away.

{% hint style="info" %}
Checkout takes digital dollars (USDC). You can add them with a card, Apple Pay or a bank transfer through MoonPay, where MoonPay operates.
{% endhint %}

## What you need

An email address, and digital dollars (USDC) to pay with. If you have none, add them by card from **Settings → Wallet → Add funds**, or send USDC on Solana from any exchange or wallet app that sells it. There is nothing to install.

## How your payment is handled

* Sellers never see your payment details.
* If a product has a refund window, your payment is protected during it. Read more in [Refunds and protection](refunds-and-protection.md).
* Everything you buy lives in one place in your account, with a receipt for each purchase. See [Your purchases](your-purchases.md).

## Common questions

<details>

<summary>Do I need an app?</summary>

Lid runs in your browser. Open the link, sign in with your email, and you are set.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What are digital dollars?</summary>

Money that works like dollars and moves over the internet in seconds. On Lid they are USDC: one USDC is one US dollar. The price you see is the price you pay.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I pay with my card?</summary>

Yes. Add funds with a card, Apple Pay or a bank transfer through MoonPay, where MoonPay operates, and pay in digital dollars (USDC).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Do I need to verify my identity to pay by card?</summary>

Yes, with MoonPay, the first time. MoonPay asks for your name, a photo of an ID document and a selfie or a quick face check before your first purchase. That check is MoonPay's, and it follows MoonPay's rules in your country. Paying with USDC you already have needs no check.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does paying by card cost more?</summary>

MoonPay charges its own fee for a card payment: up to 4.5%, with a minimum fee on small amounts, plus a network fee. You see every fee before you confirm. The details are on [MoonPay's pricing page](https://www.moonpay.com/legal/pricing_disclosure).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Is my purchase tied to one device?</summary>

Your purchases live in your account. Sign in with the same email from any device and everything is there.

</details>
