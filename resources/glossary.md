# Glossary

The handful of terms you will meet inside Lid, in plain words.

## Digital dollars

The money you earn and spend on Lid; each digital dollar is worth one US dollar.

## Commission

The share of a sale, set by the seller between 1% and 50%, that goes to the person whose link led to the purchase.

## Share link

Your personal link to a product; when someone buys through it, you earn the commission.

## Payout

The money that lands in your account the moment a sale happens, automatically.

## Refund window

A period the seller chooses during which a buyer can ask for their money back.

## Challenge

A competition with a prize. The prize sits safely to one side while people compete; when the poster approves the winners, each winner's share is locked in and they claim it from their dashboard. Challenges were called bounties until August 2026.

## Campaign

A reward pool that pays sharers for verified results, like signups or sales. Anyone can generate their own link and earn from the pool when their visitors deliver the result.

## Course

A product made of lessons that buyers unlock when they purchase.

## Membership

A paid space your buyers subscribe to: a feed for your posts, a community forum, a calendar, and 1:1 bookings, billed on a recurring subscription.

## Service

Work you sell with protected payment: the money is held until the buyer confirms the work was delivered.

## Lida

The Lid assistant, built into the app. It helps you set things up and find answers.
