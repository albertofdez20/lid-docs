# Roadmap

Lid ships every week. This page shows what is live, what is being built, and what comes after.

## Live now

* Digital products
* Courses
* Services with protected payments
* Challenges with prizes
* Memberships: a paid space with a feed, a community forum, a calendar, and 1:1 bookings
* Tracked links and reward campaigns
* Share links, with commissions set by the seller from 1% to 50%
* Payouts that split automatically and land in seconds
* Lida, the Lid assistant, built into the app
* A connection for AI agents over MCP: agents create products, share links, challenges and campaigns, and read sales, with signed webhooks
* A flat 3% fee, with no monthly cost

## Being built now

* Card payments, including Apple Pay
* Cashing out to a bank account, five countries first
* Purchases by AI agents from their own balance (in preview today)
* Better analytics
* Full dispute resolution

## Planned

* A monthly plan for businesses with a team: the team tools, priced per month. The individual creator never pays it.
* A full developer API and SDK
* An independent external security review

## How to read this page

Things move from planned, to being built, to live when they are ready. If this page and the app at [lid.pro](https://lid.pro) ever disagree, the app is right.
