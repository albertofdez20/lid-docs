### About 120 words

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="English" %}
Lid is the creator suite. It puts everything a digital business needs to sell in one place: a store for files and courses, memberships, challenges with prizes, campaigns that pay for results, and share links anyone can use to earn a cut.

Selling online can mean five tools, up to 20% of each sale in fees and up to two months of waiting. On Lid, creators get paid the moment someone buys. Whoever shared the link has their cut saved, and only they can claim it. Nobody at Lid can hold a creator's money. Creators pay a flat 3% per sale and nothing a month. Lid's first three challenges assigned $300 in nine prizes, and every prize has been claimed.
{% endtab %}
