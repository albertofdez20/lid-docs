# Your own site

Your site with your brand, hosted by Lid. Your products are paid through the Lid checkout, the same 3% on every sale.

**How it works.** Open Builder in your dashboard and describe the site you want to Lida. She builds it; you change what you like; you publish it on a Lid address or on your own domain.

**What runs on it.** Your store, your memberships, your challenges and your share links, with the same checkout as your Lid page.

**Who can use it today.** We switch it on per account. If you do not see Builder in your dashboard, write to alberto@lid.pro.
