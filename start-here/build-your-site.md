# Build your site with Lida

Tell Lida what you do. She writes and designs your site, and you see it before you pay. Lida is Lid's AI. This page takes you from "I want a site" to a site that is live.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Sign in

Go to [lid.pro](https://lid.pro) and sign in with your email or with Google. Lida opens as your home. Then open **Sites** in your dashboard menu.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Start a site

Press **Create site**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Tell Lida what you do

Lida asks what your site is about. Answer in one sentence, or paste the address of the website you already have. She shows you a plan for the site before she builds it.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Name it and pick your style

Give your site a name and an address, and choose your color and your font. Then press **Create and design with Lida**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### See your site

Lida puts the site together and you see it in a preview. Only you see the preview.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Ask for changes

Tell Lida what to change in your own words: a page, a section, the style or the text. You can attach an image, or speak instead of typing. Each change shows in the preview.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Put it live

Press **Publish** when you like what you see. Lida never publishes for you.

Putting a site live is paid, per site. You see the price in the Builder before you pay, and on [lid.pro](https://lid.pro).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Use your own domain

Your site starts on a Lid address. In the site's **Domain** tab you can connect a domain you already own. Once the site is published you can also buy a domain right there, and Lid connects it for you.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## What your site comes with

* Your own domain
* A WhatsApp button
* Your address, your hours and directions
* A layout that looks right on a phone
* Changes when you want them
* Your style

## After your site is live

* **Change it any time.** Open **Sites**, press **Open in the Builder** on your site and tell Lida what to change. You see it in the preview and you decide when to publish.
* **Turn pieces on or off.** In the site's settings you choose what the site shows and sells: products, courses, challenges, memberships, a contact form or a waitlist. Each change reaches your site in under a minute, without publishing again.
* **Read your messages.** Messages from the contact form and emails from the waitlist arrive in your site panel. The waitlist exports as a CSV file.
* **Invite someone to help.** In the **People** tab you can invite up to 5 editors by email. Editors can change the site and publish it. The money, the domain and the site's admin stay yours. An invitation expires after 14 days if nobody accepts it.

The full reference is on [Your own site](../sell-on-lid/your-own-site.md).
